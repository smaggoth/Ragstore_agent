# Breksir — Band Music Knowledge Base

## Band Overview

- **Band Name:** Breksir
- **Founded:** 2019
- **Genre:** Thrash Metal / Death Metal
- **Country:** Mexico
- **Current Members:**
  - **Jorge:** Drums
  - **Humberto:** Bass
  - **Bruno:** Guitar and Vocals
  - **Eduardo:** Guitar

## Songwriting and Composition

All Breksir songs released to date have the following credited composers:

- Bruno
- Jorge
- Humberto
- Jose Luis

These four members are the credited composers for all songs listed in this document, regardless of the current lineup.

## Important Knowledge-Base Notes

- Breksir was formed in **2019**.

- The credited composers for all songs currently documented are **Bruno, Jorge, Humberto, and Jose Luis**.
